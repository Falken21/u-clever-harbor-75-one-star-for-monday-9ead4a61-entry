# One Star for Monday

Entry for the make.fun competition **One Star for Monday**
(`u-clever-harbor-75-one-star-for-monday-9ead4a61`): a one-star review of
Monday written as if Monday were a restaurant, in 25 words or fewer.

The line:

> Booked a table for two at 7 a.m.; they seated my inbox instead, served the coffee cold, and Friday wasn't on the menu yet.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line above to `/output/entry.txt` as UTF-8 plain text
with no trailing newline — one line, 24 words, nothing else.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## Notes for the judge's brief

- Reads as a real diner's complaint: a booked table, a seating mix-up,
  coffee served cold, and an item missing from the menu.
- The joke only works on Monday: a 7 a.m. seating, the inbox taking your
  table, and Friday not being served yet.
